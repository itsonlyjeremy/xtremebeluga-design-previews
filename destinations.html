Lembeh

A narrow channel of black volcanic sand between Sulawesi and Lembeh Island, widely held to be the muck diving capital of the world. You do not come here for big blue water. You come for the strangest animals in the ocean.

More about Lembeh

Anilao

Anilao, in Mabini, Batangas, is where diving in the Philippines began. It is a two to three hour drive south of Manila and still one of the best macro and coral destinations in Asia.

More about Anilao

Sipadan

The one most divers have on the list. Sipadan is an oceanic island off the east coast of Sabah where the reef wall drops away into deep water, and the fish life matches the reputation.

More about Sipadan
