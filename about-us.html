About us

Where every dive is an adventure

We are folks from all walks of life, brought together by our love for the sea and belief that scuba diving trips must be delivered with the highest quality of safety and professionalism. Combining our enthusiasm, and our years of experience, we want to share the beauty of the sea with you. Our greatest satisfaction comes in seeing you experience the wonders of the sea and learn the necessary skills to explore the sea safely.

Xtreme Beluga aims to be your one stop dive center for dive courses and dive holidays.

Based in Singapore and Malaysia and established in 2012, this company is the brain-child of 4 marine sports enthusiasts who understand that good training do make a difference between a mediocre weekend of diving.

We take pride in delivery high service standards, safety procedures as well as professional teaching, Xtreme Beluga will strive to ensure your course and/or holidays are enjoyable and meaningful.
